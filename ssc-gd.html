Exam overview

What is SSC GD?

Constable (GD) in CAPFs / Rifleman (GD). The recruitment is conducted according to the official notice issued for each cycle.

Which posts can you get?

Constable (GD) in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB and SSF, plus Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles, as listed in the relevant notice. Force allocation depends on merit, preference, vacancies and rules.

Basic educational qualification

Generally Matriculation / 10th pass from a recognised board. Age limits, relaxations, physical standards and other conditions are set by the specific recruitment notice.

Baseline: 10th pass

Selection process

Computer-based examination, Physical Standard Test, Physical Efficiency Test, medical examination and document verification. The recruiting forces conduct the physical and medical stages as per the notice.

What is generally studied?

Computer-based exam generally covers General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Knowledge & General Awareness, Elementary Mathematics and English/Hindi. Physical tests follow for shortlisted candidates.

Important: Force-wise physical standards, race/events, age and domicile-related conditions must be checked in the latest SSC GD notification. These are general guidance notes, not a substitute for the latest official notification. Meeting minimum qualification does not guarantee selection.
