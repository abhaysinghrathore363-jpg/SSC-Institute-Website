Exam overview

What is Delhi Police?

Delhi Police recruitment examinations. The recruitment is conducted according to the official notice issued for each cycle.

Which posts can you get?

Depending on the recruitment notice: Constable (Executive) Male/Female, Head Constable (Ministerial), Head Constable (AWO/TPO), and Sub-Inspector (Executive) through SSC CPO. Not every post is open in every recruitment cycle.

Basic educational qualification

Constable and Head Constable posts generally have 10+2-level educational requirements, while SI generally requires graduation. Some posts require a valid driving licence or specific computer/typing skills. Exact conditions are post-specific.

Baseline: Post-specific

Selection process

The process varies by post and may include computer-based examination, physical endurance/measurement tests, typing or computer skill tests, trade tests, medical examination and document verification.

What is generally studied?

Written exam subjects commonly include reasoning, general awareness/current affairs, numerical ability and language/computer-related knowledge as prescribed for the post.

Important: Delhi Police recruitment is not one single exam. Always select the correct post notification and check its qualification, physical standards and skill-test requirements. These are general guidance notes, not a substitute for the latest official notification. Meeting minimum qualification does not guarantee selection.
