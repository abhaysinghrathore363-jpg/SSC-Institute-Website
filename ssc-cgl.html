Exam overview

What is SSC CGL?

Combined Graduate Level. The recruitment is conducted according to the official notice issued for each cycle.

Which posts can you get?

Assistant Section Officer (ASO), Inspector posts in departments such as Income Tax / Central Excise / Examiner / Preventive Officer (post-wise conditions apply), Auditor, Accountant, Tax Assistant, and other notified Group B and C posts.

Basic educational qualification

Generally a bachelor’s degree from a recognised university. Certain specialised posts may require specific subjects, qualifications or experience. Age limits vary by post/category in the notification.

Baseline: Graduation

Selection process

Tier-I computer-based examination, Tier-II computer-based examination and post-specific requirements such as skill/DEST where applicable. Final allocation depends on merit, preferences, vacancies and eligibility.

What is generally studied?

Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English and General Awareness; Tier-II includes modules/papers as prescribed for the post and year.

Important: The exact post list, age limit, syllabus, paper structure and skill-test requirements must be checked in the current SSC CGL notice. These are general guidance notes, not a substitute for the latest official notification. Meeting minimum qualification does not guarantee selection.
