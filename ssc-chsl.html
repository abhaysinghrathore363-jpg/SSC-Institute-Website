Exam overview

What is SSC CHSL?

Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level. The recruitment is conducted according to the official notice issued for each cycle.

Which posts can you get?

Lower Divisional Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Data Entry Operator (DEO) and other DEO grades/posts as specified in the notification.

Basic educational qualification

Generally 12th standard pass or equivalent from a recognised board. Some DEO posts may require Science stream with Mathematics at 12th level. Age limit and relaxations are notification-specific.

Baseline: 12th pass

Selection process

Tier-I computer-based exam followed by Tier-II computer-based exam, including applicable skill test / typing test modules. Exact modules depend on the post and current notice.

What is generally studied?

English Language, General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude and General Awareness; Tier-II includes additional sections and skill/typing requirements as notified.

Important: Post-specific educational requirements, typing/DEST standards, age limits and exam pattern can change; check the latest notice. These are general guidance notes, not a substitute for the latest official notification. Meeting minimum qualification does not guarantee selection.
