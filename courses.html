Before you apply

Know the difference between exam and job.

What does the exam decide?

The examination is a recruitment process. Clearing a written paper alone does not automatically guarantee a particular post. Final selection can depend on later stages, merit, category rules, preferences, vacancies, medical/physical standards and document verification.

Where should you verify?

Always read the current official recruitment notice before applying. Eligibility, age limits, syllabus, exam stages and post lists may be revised.
